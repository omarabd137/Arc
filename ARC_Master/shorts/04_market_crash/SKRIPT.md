# ARC Short #04 – "Should You Sell Your Stocks Right Now?" (~40 s)

Freigegeben von Omar ("das ist mal perfekt").
Titel (passt 1:1 zum Hook): **Should You Sell Your Stocks Right Now?**

## Voiceover (Brian, eleven_v4)
S1 "[dramatically] Should you sell your stocks right now?"
S2 "[serious] On Thursday, two and a half trillion dollars vanished from Wall Street. In one day. Nvidia alone lost four hundred fifty billion."
S3 "Why? Fear that new rules could slow down AI. And a lot of people had bet on AI with borrowed money. When prices dropped, they were forced to sell. That's why it crashed so fast."
S4 "[deep voice] So what's the smart move? History has three rules."
S5 "[serious] One: don't panic-sell. The biggest jumps usually come right after the biggest drops. Miss them, and you lose years of gains."
S6 "Two: don't go all in either. Smart money buys slowly, a little every month, so it never has to guess the bottom."
S7 "Three: only invest money you won't need for years. Then a crash isn't a disaster. It's a sale."
S8 "[deep voice] Not financial advice. Just how crashes made people rich instead of poor."
S9 "So... are you holding, buying, or selling?"
